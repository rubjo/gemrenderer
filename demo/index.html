gemrenderer · MIT · framework-free

Real gemstone cuts,
ray-traced in the browser

From trilliant and baguette to pear, emerald cut, heart, cabochon and more — each stone shows off a different cut. Under the hood, each pixel traces a light path through the stone, giving realistic reflection, refraction and dispersion. Drag any stone to spin it.
